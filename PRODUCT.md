# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Scantap serves independent business owners, location managers, reputation teams, and larger multi-location organizations. Owners need a fast weekly overview; managers need a focused location queue; authorized responders need to draft and publish replies without receiving unrelated administrative access.

## Product Purpose

Scantap combines NFC and QR review products with a reputation workspace. Customers receive a direct, neutral path to a business's configured review destination. Business teams monitor reviews, reply, compare locations, manage devices, and understand what changed without turning reputation management into another reporting project.

## Positioning

Scantap connects the physical moment when a customer is ready to share an experience with an operational review workspace that keeps taps, review-page opens, published reviews, replies, and private feedback honest and distinct.

## Operating Context

The product is used at counters, exits, reception desks, job closeouts, and other in-person handoffs. Business users work from a responsive web application organized by organization and location. Google Business Profile is the primary review source. Other providers join through capability-labeled integrations that may be native, read-only, or link-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Organizations can contain multiple locations and members.
- Organization roles and location assignments jointly determine access.
- Owners and administrators manage people, locations, policies, and integrations.
- Managers can operate approved locations; responders can publish replies where permitted; analysts and viewers remain read-only.
- Organization policies provide defaults, while permitted location policies may override them.
- Google is the first native review integration. Provider connectivity depends on credentials, API approval, and each provider's terms.
- Provider credentials remain server-only and encrypted before persistence.
- Cached Google Business Profile content expires within 30 calendar days, and provider-supplied summary values stay distinct from Scantap event metrics.
- A publish action is a specific user decision; Google moderation states remain pending, rejected, or published until confirmed by Google.
- The public dashboard remains an explicitly labeled demonstration. Authenticated customer routes use persisted data and protected sessions.

## Brand Commitments

The public brand is Scantap. The incumbent Signal Ledger identity is binding: cobalt blue, graphite, white, and cool gray; crisp workhorse typography; compact operational controls; restrained motion; direct, human language; and no decorative complexity that slows a task.

## Evidence on Hand

- The current repository contains the production marketing website, product imagery, and an interactive sample dashboard.
- Existing dashboard reviews, locations, metrics, and devices are fictional demonstration data.
- No customer testimonials, production review records, connected Google accounts, or verified performance benchmarks are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Show the next useful action before adding another metric.
2. Enforce permissions in the database as well as the interface.
3. Label every integration by what it can actually do.
4. Keep event meanings distinct and claims supportable.
5. Offer the same neutral public-review path to every customer; never sentiment-gate, incentivize, suppress, or fabricate reviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web application must support keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced motion, responsive reflow, readable contrast, and clear loading, empty, error, disabled, stale, and permission-denied states.

Scope: `/control`; visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: Scantap's owner and future trusted platform operators. Their recurring job is to see portfolio health, identify the few accounts or systems needing intervention, and move into the correct customer context without weakening tenant boundaries.

Primary task: understand business health within seconds, then resolve a prioritized operating item. Proof comes from real database counts and dated events. The product walkthrough is explicitly illustrative and never contributes to business metrics.

Constraints: cross-organization data is server-only behind a separate platform-staff authorization boundary; no customer can reach this surface through organization roles. Preserve the Signal Ledger palette, workhorse type, compact controls, semantic states, responsive reflow, and honest metric definitions.

Direction: Operations control deck. A portfolio pulse strip anchors the first viewport; a dominant action ledger and customer portfolio follow; the walkthrough sits in a dedicated training bay. The memorable moment is the clean separation between platform-wide operating signals and customer-scoped review work.

Unresolved: billing remains manual until a payment provider is connected; revenue is zero until entered into customer account records; live provider health requires credentials and sync activity.

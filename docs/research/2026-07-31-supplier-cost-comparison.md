## Required physical tests

1. Encode an HTTPS test URL on every candidate tag and verify read/write/lock behavior.
2. Test NFC and QR on representative iPhone and Android devices.
3. Measure read reliability through the chosen acrylic, paper, laminate, vinyl, and adhesive stack.
4. Test on wood, glass, painted counter, plastic, and metal; use an on-metal tag where conductive surfaces are possible.
5. Confirm the printed QR meets a defined minimum size and contrast and remains scannable under counter lighting.
6. Run 100 tap/scan cycles per sample and record failures separately by NFC and QR.
7. Clean each product with likely retail cleaners and reinspect face, adhesive, curl, delamination, and NFC performance.
8. For adhesive plates, record removal damage and residue; do not call the bond removable without evidence.
9. Record exact active labor for artwork, encoding, QA, packaging, activation, and support.
10. Compute landed variable cost before accepting the pilot price:

```text
landed variable cost =
  product/substrate
  + NFC tag/card
  + printing/finish
  + QR/variable-data work
  + shipping/tax/duty allocation
  + packaging
  + payment fee
  + expected replacement allowance
  + active fulfillment/activation labor
```
